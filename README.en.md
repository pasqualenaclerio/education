# education

[🇮🇹 Italiano](README.md) | 🇬🇧 English

This educational repository collects **lecture notes, teaching materials, software, examples, and exercises** used during my lessons.

The purpose of the project is to organize the material developed for teaching activities in a single place, making it easy to access for students and reusable by teachers, enthusiasts, and anyone interested in the topics covered.

The repository is intended to be a **continuously evolving project**: its contents may be updated, corrected, expanded, or reorganized over time.

---

## Repository contents

The main structure of the project is organized by subject area and discipline:

```text
education/
├── ARDUINO/
├── TEEA/
├── TTIM/
├── README.md
└── README.en.md
```

### `ARDUINO/`

The **ARDUINO** folder contains projects, examples, and programs developed using the Arduino platform.

It may include, for example:

- sketches and programs;
- examples involving sensors and actuators;
- data acquisition and visualization;
- laboratory exercises;
- small measurement, control, and automation systems;
- supporting material for projects carried out during lessons.

These projects are mainly intended for educational purposes and are designed to connect theoretical concepts with practical laboratory activities.

---

### `TEEA/`

**TEEA** stands for **Tecnologie elettrico-elettroniche e applicazioni**, which can be translated as **Electrical and Electronic Technologies and Applications**.

The subject covers the study and practical application of the main concepts of electrical engineering and electronics, with particular attention to components, circuits, electrical and electronic measurements, and their practical applications.

The folder contains **lecture notes, exercises, examples, programs, and teaching materials** for:

- **3rd-year MAT**
- **4th-year MAT**
- **5th-year MAT**

where **MAT** stands for **Manutenzione e Assistenza Tecnica** (*Maintenance and Technical Assistance*), an Italian vocational education pathway focused on the management, installation, maintenance, and testing of equipment, systems, and technical installations.

---

### `TTIM/`

**TTIM** stands for **Tecnologie e tecniche di installazione e manutenzione**, which can be translated as **Installation and Maintenance Technologies and Techniques**.

The subject focuses on the methods and techniques used for the installation, maintenance, and testing of technical systems and equipment. Particular attention is given to troubleshooting, diagnostics, technical documentation, safety, and maintenance planning.

The folder contains **lecture notes, exercises, examples, technical documentation, and teaching materials** for:

- **3rd-year MAT**
- **4th-year MAT**
- **5th-year MAT**

within the **Manutenzione e Assistenza Tecnica (MAT)** vocational pathway.

## Project purpose

This repository was created primarily to support lessons and laboratory activities.

Its main goals are to:

- organize teaching materials clearly;
- provide students with lecture notes and examples used during lessons;
- preserve and version programs and projects developed over time;
- make laboratory exercises and activities easier to share;
- allow the material to be reused in other educational contexts;
- maintain documentation that is easy to update and access.
