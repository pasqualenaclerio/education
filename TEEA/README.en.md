# TEEA

[🇮🇹 Italiano](README.md) | 🇬🇧 English

**TEEA** stands for **Tecnologie elettrico-elettroniche e applicazioni** (*Electrical and Electronic Technologies and Applications*).

The subject is part of the **MAT – Manutenzione e Assistenza Tecnica** (*Maintenance and Technical Assistance*) vocational pathway and covers the fundamentals of electrical engineering and electronics, components and circuits, electrical and electronic measurements, and their practical applications.

This folder contains lecture notes, exercises, examples, and materials used during lessons and laboratory activities for **3rd-, 4th-, and 5th-year MAT classes**.

## Structure

- [`3MAT`](3MAT/) — materials for the 3rd-year MAT class
- [`4MAT`](4MAT/) — materials for the 4th-year MAT class
- [`5MAT`](5MAT/) — materials for the 5th-year MAT class
