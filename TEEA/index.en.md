# TEEA

### Electrical and Electronic Technologies and Applications

The subject covers the fundamentals of electrical engineering and electronics, components and circuits, electrical and electronic measurements, and their practical applications.

This section contains teaching materials for the **MAT – Manutenzione e Assistenza Tecnica** (*Maintenance and Technical Assistance*) vocational pathway.
