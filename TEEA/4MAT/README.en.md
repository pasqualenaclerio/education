# TEEA — 4MAT

[🇮🇹 Italiano](README.md) | 🇬🇧 English

This folder contains teaching materials for **TEEA – Electrical and Electronic Technologies and Applications** intended for the **4th-year MAT class** within the **Manutenzione e Assistenza Tecnica** (*Maintenance and Technical Assistance*) vocational pathway.

The lecture notes, exercises, examples, and projects included in this folder form part of the **teaching programme for the 4th year** and may be updated or expanded during the school year.

## Topics

Each topic with its materials: lesson notes in PDF, formula sheet, exercises and solutions (formula sheets, exercises and solutions in Italian).
